Trailhead Layout One

You picked the layout where the land climbs into the mountains. This is its second pass, with your three notes worked in: the dry side is no longer three stacked bands, the top right went to the granite and the larches, and the fog reaches over the water at the west edge. It is still a block-in for shape and place. Nothing here is final art.

The Top Right, Reclaimed

The granite range now runs to the east edge, and under it the basins step down like a staircase: a top basin, the basin under Proficiency Peak, then the lower larch basin. Larches fill all of it, thick at the bottom and sparse on the bare slab at the top.

The water is the chain you described. One creek starts in the top basin, runs through a tarn, drops over the lip in a fall, runs through the next tarn, and so on down to the valley, where it joins the meltwater from the glacier. The river is wider below each joining.

The Fog

Trees now turn grey where they turned pale green, and they do it gradually: full colour beside the lake, then a mesh of colour and grey, then flat silhouettes that pale toward the west. The fog also lies over the beach and the sea at the far west edge.

It is not right yet. The grey trees merge into slabs, where your picture has one clear rank of crowns per ridge with white between. I tuned the rule that plants them four times and it swings between a white sheet and a grey wall. I think this is the limit of rules: the ranks need to be drawn ridge by ridge, which belongs to the real drawing, not the block-in. Snags against white and eyes at Dusk come then too.

The Middle, Closer

Mount Monolith has its broad shoulders and summit crater back and stands on a rock foot above the lake's bench. The Spires are wider, so the six summits read apart. The Legacy Range is taller behind the fog (top left of the whole map).
