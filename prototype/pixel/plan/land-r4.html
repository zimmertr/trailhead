Trailhead Land Build

The land from the signed-off plan, built. This is a progress picture, not a stop: the game does not run on it yet, and the trees, fog and rock are still rough stand-ins. It is here so you can see the shape of the land early.

The Crest Is One Mass

The two cones are gone. The ground between Mount Monolith and the granite now climbs to the foot of the Spires, so the meadow no longer reaches the wall, and the glacier lies in a bowl between two rock arms.

From the top: snow gullies run up the wall to the notches, a dark gap opens where the ice meets the rock, the upper snowfield breaks into an icefall, and a tongue of blue ice runs down between moraines to a milky tarn. Base camp sits on the moraine beside the tongue, with the icefall above it.

The flower slope under camp is where the traverse will cross, with a stream to hop.

Water Finds Its Own Way

No stream is drawn by hand below its source any more. Each one runs downhill over the land, and its width comes from how much water has joined it. West of the Trailhead the lake's outflow, a spring off Mount Monolith, a brook off the Spires' arm and the glacier's meltwater join into one river, which cuts through the rainforest to the sea. There is one oxbow.

Still wrong: a few reaches run straight for too long, most of all just below where streams join. No footbridges yet where trails cross.

The Granite Steps and the Coulee

Three steps. The tarns are milky on the top step, turquoise on the second and clear on the first. Larches start at the second step. The first has aspens and ponderosa pines, and the burn lies across its east end and runs off the map.

The rock is now one grey family along the whole crest, a step paler in the granite, with less snow toward the east.

The creek runs east of the Trailhead's rise and leaves through a coulee with basalt walls, which deepens as the dry tableland rises round it.
