What is rough on purpose

  • This is a block-in for shape, size and place. The final art comes after you choose.
  • The volcano's flanks are smooth and even, so it still leans toward Fuji in A and C. The final one gets uneven shoulders, real icefalls and rock bands.
  • The yellow hills read as striped mounds. They get drawn as rolling Palouse hills.
  • The white ledges on the Spires are evenly spaced dashes. They become real snow ledges and cracks.
  • The west range (left of About Lake) is untouched in all three. It is still taller on the map than the Spires.
  • The base camp scene still shows the old towers. It gets redrawn to match the Spires you pick.
  • Only A was checked at dusk.
