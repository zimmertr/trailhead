The Three Mountains Up Close

Walking It West to East

  • Fog and open sea, then the beach with its sea stacks and low bluffs.
  • Rainforest, thinning to bare snags where the fog swallows the west edge. The Legacy Range stands pale and far over it.
  • The lake's bench: Reflection Lake and flower parkland under Mount Monolith. The river drops off the bench in a waterfall and runs out through the forest to the sea.
  • The meadow at the Trailhead, under the crest. Above it the headwall, base camp on its shelf, the glacier and the Spires.
  • The larch basins: the lower one thick with larches, then a granite headwall with a cascade, then the upper one of bare slab and tarns under Proficiency Peak.
  • Pines and aspen, the old burn in the bottom corner, then sage, scabland benches and the Palouse hills at the far edge.
  • No wet country touches dry country. The skyline holds four kinds of mountain and nothing else: the snowy crest (with the Legacy Range as its far cousin), one volcano, the granite, and the dry hills.

What Is Weak or Rough

  • Every mountain is a shape with stand-in shading. Mount Monolith's rock ribs are smooth blades and its icefalls are dashes. The Spires' ledge snow is rows of similar dashes.
  • Proficiency Peak is drawn 164 by 142, larger than the 150 by 120 we wrote down, so that its summit breaks the skyline. The granite behind it was lowered for the same reason. Those back peaks are still too alike.
  • The Legacy Range is small and half lost in the fog. It may not be worth its place.
  • The bottom centre is a large quiet meadow, about a quarter of the ground. Either something lives there (the elk herd, the bear) or the map could be less tall.
  • The meltwater stream from the glacier runs the whole height of the map and is the strongest line on it. It could end in a pond, or join the river.
  • The rainforest is one even block. The coast's bluffs barely show and the river's notch through them is not drawn.
  • The lake is small beside the volcano behind it, and does not mirror it yet.
  • Not in yet: animals, joke signs, footbridges, clouds, Dusk, the eerie west edge.
